Who We Are

Building minds, building the nation

Founded in 1998, Sunrise Public School began with a vision — to bring quality, affordable CBSE education to the families of Darbhanga. Today, we stand as one of the region's most trusted institutions, with 1,800+ students and a team of 85 dedicated educators.

Our philosophy is simple: every child is unique. We combine strong academics with sports, cultural activities, and life-skills education so our students excel in every field they choose.

From our first batch of 40 students to thousands of successful alumni — the Sunrise journey continues with the same passion.
